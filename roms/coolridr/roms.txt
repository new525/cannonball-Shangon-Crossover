Place the Cool Riders ROMs in this folder: files of MAME set coolridr,
"Cool Riders" (MAME 0.264). Only the sound side is used (the game's
cover of Hang-On's theme), so only these 13 files are needed; the set's
ten 4 MB sprite ROMs (mpr-17640 to mpr-17649) can be left out.
Files are found by CRC32 first, then by name.

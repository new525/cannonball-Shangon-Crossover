Place the OutRunners ROMs in this folder: files of MAME set orunnersj,
"OutRunners (Japan)" (MAME 0.264).
- Its music (the covers of OutRun's music) needs only the first 3 files
  below; they are the same in orunners and orunnersu, so those sets work
  for the music too.
- Its courses and car (OUTRUNNERS FULL COURSE, OUTRUNNERS KENYA, MAD
  POWER) need every file, of orunnersj: the program's hooks were read from
  the Japanese version's code.
Files are found by CRC32 first, then by name, as loose files or in the .zip
files in this folder: MAME's orunnersj.zip with its parent orunners.zip
(orunnersj.zip alone holds only its own two program ROMs), or a merged
orunners.zip. Loose files are used first.

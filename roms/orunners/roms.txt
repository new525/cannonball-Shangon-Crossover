-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Place the OutRunners ROMs in this folder: files of MAME set orunners,
orunnersu or orunnersj, "OutRunners" (MAME 0.264). Only the sound side
is used (the game's covers of OutRun's music), so only these 3 files are
needed; they are the same in all three sets.
Files are found by CRC32 first, then by name.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
epr-15550.ic31     CRC32 0205d2ed  (soundcpu, the sound Z80's program)
mpr-15551.ic1      CRC32 4894bc73  (sega, MultiPCM samples)
mpr-15552.ic2      CRC32 1c4b5e73  (sega, MultiPCM samples)
